A Tarnished in the Realm of Code

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at the University of Guelph. While a lot of people try to avoid the low-level stuff, I genuinely enjoy figuring out how things work under the hood.

For me, computer science is the perfect mix of strict logic and creative problem-solving. Lately, I've been spending most of my time diving into foundational software engineering, low-level memory management in C, and figuring out the math that actually makes machine learning work. I firmly believe that to build cool stuff in AI or game dev, you have to understand the bare-metal operations first. You can't cast high-level spells if you don't understand the basic mana mechanics.

Currently, I'm looking for a summer research position within the CS department where I can take this theoretical knowledge and apply it to some real-world problems.
