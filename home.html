Professional Summary

As I progress through my early university career, I am actively seeking summer research positions within the computer science department where I can apply my theoretical knowledge to practical, cutting-edge technical challenges.

During my time at the university, I have immersed myself deeply in the foundational concepts of software engineering, low-level hardware architecture, and complex data structures.
